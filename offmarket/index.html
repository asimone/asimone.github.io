Code basis: 2025 NYS Uniform Code

  • In force since Dec 31, 2025, with no transition period left. A January 2027 permit application must use the 2025 editions.
  • Existing Building, Building, Fire, Fuel Gas, Mechanical and Plumbing Codes of NYS, plus the 2025 Energy Code.

Working assumptions to confirm or break early

  • Nonseparated mixed occupancy; no sprinklers; no corridor rating
  • Two restrooms, both accessible; coldworking room in a former restroom
  • On-site oxygen generation for torches, little stored oxygen
  • Fire department key box; no new sidewalk or parking repave
