Weekly & Site Services

Recurring services that apply across multiple days of the event
Security
  • Registration Building (Accueil): 24-hour presence starting Friday, Aug. 7 (10:00 AM)
  • 1540: 24-hour presence starting Sunday, Aug. 9 (9:00 AM)
  • Caregivers/Dispensary: 24-hour presence (near the Accueil) starting Friday, Aug. 7 (10:00 AM)
  • Fire Prevention Specialist: 24-hour presence, on call
  • Community Brigade: Monday to Thursday, 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM and 5:00 PM to 10:00 PM / Friday 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM / Saturday 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM and 5:00 PM to 10:00 PM
  • Night Watch: Sunday to Saturday, 9:00 PM to 2:00 AM / Friday August 14th 9:00 PM to 3:00 AM
  • CALACS: Thursday, 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM
  • Tandem: Monday to Saturday, 6:00 PM to midnight
Auberge Showers
  • Payment by credit card reader on site (Can$6.00)
  • Access cards on sale at the Market (Can$6.00) (bundles available)
  • Open 24/7, with temporary closures for cleaning
Faubourg Showers
  • Payment by credit card reader on site (Can$6.00)
  • Open from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM with temporary closures during maintenance (when required)
Garbage, Returnable Containers and Recycling
  • In-game collection (Farfouilleurs): Monday to Saturday, 11:00 AM to 12:00 PM at the Faubourg Préau
  • Site-wide collection of returnable containers: Monday to Saturday, 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM
  • Garbage collection from identified sectors: Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM
  • Garbage drop-off by participants from other sectors in containers directly on site
  • Recycling drop-off by all participants in the container at the P1 parking lot

  • Friday, Aug. 7: closed
